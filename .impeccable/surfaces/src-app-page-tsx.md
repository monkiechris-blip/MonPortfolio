# Surface: portfolio home (single page)

Mode: Experience (portfolio), user took the category standard (canon) after rejecting the amber world. Code-led.
Audience: French master admission committees, recruiters. Job: in < 1 min know who, what was shipped, how to contact / get CV.
Constraints: French only; no KWatt; no birth date/place, BP address or certificate. Photo is 230x290: display small and crisp only.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The software-engineer portfolio played straight at full craft: calm, light, precise. It refuses decoration; credibility comes from structure, real facts and typographic care.

OWN-WORLD: off-white ground #FAFAF9, white surfaces, hairline borders #E7E5E4, near-black ink #0C0C0E, secondary #57534E, one navy accent #1E3A8A for primary action, links and the award. Geist Sans throughout, Geist Mono only for dates and stack. Radius 12–16 on cards, 10 on controls. Soft neutral shadows only on hover.

STORY: name, role and aim in one glance with an identity card; proof (SkillMap 1er prix, IMAKO, majors); projects with need / role / result and code links; parcours timeline; skills; contact via email, WhatsApp, GitHub, CV.

FIRST VIEWPORT: sticky header (wordmark, links, CV button). Left: name as h1, role line, two-sentence summary, primary "Voir les projets" + secondary "Télécharger le CV", three proof facts in one row. Right: identity card with round portrait, formation, objective, location, languages, contact icons.

FORM: canon (category standard), chosen by user. seed:6a264d0bbfa12fc6 (original roll; canon taken on redesign).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
